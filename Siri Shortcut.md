# Siri Shortcut
Siri Shortcuts are an easy way to automate tasks on your Apple devices; I'm a fan, so
this is my preferred way to download media using this project.

Once you add this shortcut, you can add it to your share sheet. Then it is accessible
once you click "share" on any video link in YouTube or Instagram or any future supported platform,
all in about 3 steps, you can get the video saved without leaving the current app.

Siri Shortcuts can't be converted to code, sharing is limited to iCloud links.  
Siri Shortcut iCloud links are immutable, meaning once you create a link, it exists
for that version of your shortcut, and future updates to the shortcut will not apply to 
the shortcut in the iCloud link. Instead, a new iCloud link has to be created for the new version.

To solve this, I have decided to add a list of iCloud links in this file, in descending order
(the latest iCloud link is at the top).
